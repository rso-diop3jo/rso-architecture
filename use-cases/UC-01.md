# UC-01 Learner signs in with Google

**Actor:** person who is learning (learner) \
**Goal:** sign in to the app (a first-time learner is registered automatically) \
**Preconditions:** Learner has Google account **&** Learner is not signed in \
**Trigger:** Learner wants to start or continue learning.

---

### Main flow (returning learner):

1. Learner opens the login page.
2. Learner clicks "Log in with Google".
3. Google shows its sign-in page.
4. Learner chooses their Google account and signs in.
5. If this is the first time, Google asks the learner to allow the app to see their name and email. Learner allows.
6. Google sends the learner back to the app.
7. The app finds the learner profile for this Google account.
8. The app signs the learner in.
9. The app shows the learner their list of worlds, with the daily tasks they should do.

A first-time learner follows alternate flow 7a. It is also a success.

---

### Alternate flows:

**3a. Google's sign-in page cannot be reached** (at step 3)

1. The learner's browser cannot load Google's page, so the learner stays outside the app.
2. The learner is not signed in and no profile is created. The learner can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**5a. Learner cancels or denies at Google** (at step 4 or 5)

1. Google sends the learner back to the app without approval.
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in was not completed. The learner is not signed in and no profile is created. Use case ends.

**6a. The app cannot verify the sign-in with Google** (at step 6)

1. Google sends the learner back, but the app cannot confirm the sign-in with Google (no answer in time, or an error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in is temporarily unavailable. The learner is not signed in and no profile is created. The learner can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**7a. New learner: no profile exists yet** (at step 7)

1. The app finds no profile for this Google account. It registers the learner automatically and creates a new learner profile from the name and email Google provides.
2. The app starts preparing the learner's starting game state, which is the starting character without equipment and empty progress. It is not ready yet.
3. The app signs the learner in (step 8) and continues at 9a instead of step 9.

**7b. The app fails to look up the profile** (at step 7)

1. The app could not read the learner profile (internal error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in failed and the learner should try again. The learner is not signed in. The learner can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**7c. The app fails to create the profile** (at 7a.1)

1. The app received everything from Google but could not save the new profile (internal error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in failed and the learner should try again. The learner is not signed in and no profile is created. The learner can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**9a. The starting game state is not ready** (at step 9, or after 7a)

1. The profile exists, but the starting game state is not ready: it was only just requested (new learner), it is taking longer than expected, or it could not be created.
2. The app shows the learner the welcome screen with the message "Your game is being prepared". The learner does not have to register again.
3. When the starting game state is ready, the learner can enter a game.
4. Preparing the starting game state again, or twice, never creates a second profile or a second starting state.

---

### Result:

The learner is signed in and a learner profile exists for this Google account. A returning learner sees their list of worlds with the daily tasks. A new learner sees the welcome screen while the starting game state is being prepared; it will exist shortly.

---

### Business events made during the use case:

**LearnerRegistered**

- Created at 7a.1, as soon as the new learner profile is saved (not at the end of the use case).
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that builds the starting game state (7a.2). This is why preparing the state is async.
- Not created in the main flow or in 3a, 5a, 6a, 7b and 7c, because nothing was registered. In 9a it already exists.

**StartingGameStateCreated**

- Created when the starting game state is ready.
- Reacts to it: the app, which then lets the learner enter a game (9a.3).

Signing in itself creates no business event: other services react to `LearnerRegistered` and to answered questions, not to logins.

---

### Metrics produced in this use case:

- Sign-in success rate (successful vs failed sign-ins).
- Failed sign-ins by reason: cancelled or denied (5a), Google check failed (6a), profile lookup failed (7b), profile not saved (7c). 3a is invisible to the app: the learner's browser never reaches us.
- Registration success rate (new learners registered vs failed with 7c).
- Time from 7a.1 until the starting game state is ready (how long learners see "Your game is being prepared") - used to see if the speed of preparation and setup needs to be sped up.

"Sign-in failed" is a metric or log, not a business event: no other service reacts to it.
