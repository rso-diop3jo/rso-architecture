# UC-01 Traveler signs in with Google

**Actor:** person who travels (traveler) \
**Goal:** sign in to the app (a first-time traveler is registered automatically) \
**Preconditions:** Traveler has Google account **&** Traveler is not signed in \
**Trigger:** Traveler wants to start or continue their adventure.

---

### Main flow (returning traveler):

1. Traveler opens the login page.
2. Traveler clicks "Log in with Google".
3. Google shows its sign-in page.
4. Traveler chooses their Google account and signs in.
5. If this is the first time, Google asks the traveler to allow the app to see their name and email. Traveler allows.
6. Google sends the traveler back to the app.
7. The app finds the traveler profile for this Google account.
8. The app signs the traveler in.
9. The app shows the traveler their group, their campaign and today's quests. A traveler without a campaign is offered to start one (UC-02).

A first-time traveler follows alternate flow 7a. It is also a success.

---

### Alternate flows:

**3a. Google's sign-in page cannot be reached** (at step 3)

1. The traveler's browser cannot load Google's page, so the traveler stays outside the app.
2. The traveler is not signed in and no profile is created. The traveler can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**5a. Traveler cancels or denies at Google** (at step 4 or 5)

1. Google sends the traveler back to the app without approval.
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in was not completed. The traveler is not signed in and no profile is created. Use case ends.

**6a. The app cannot verify the sign-in with Google** (at step 6)

1. Google sends the traveler back, but the app cannot confirm the sign-in with Google (no answer in time, or an error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in is temporarily unavailable. The traveler is not signed in and no profile is created. The traveler can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**7a. New traveler: no profile exists yet** (at step 7)

1. The app finds no profile for this Google account. It registers the traveler automatically and creates a new traveler profile from the name and email Google provides.
2. The app starts preparing the traveler's starting state: a solo group in which the traveler is the only member, and then a starting character without equipment and with empty progress in that group. It is not ready yet.
3. The app signs the traveler in (step 8) and continues at 9a instead of step 9.

**7b. The app fails to look up the profile** (at step 7)

1. The app could not read the traveler profile (internal error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in failed and the traveler should try again. The traveler is not signed in. The traveler can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**7c. The app fails to create the profile** (at 7a.1)

1. The app received everything from Google but could not save the new profile (internal error).
2. The app shows the login page with a message that sign-in failed and the traveler should try again. The traveler is not signed in and no profile is created. The traveler can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**9a. The starting state is not ready** (at step 9, or after 7a)

1. The profile exists, but the solo group or the starting character is not ready: it was only just requested (new traveler), it is taking longer than expected, or it could not be created.
2. The app shows the traveler the welcome screen with the message "Your adventure is being prepared". The traveler does not have to register again.
3. When both the solo group and the starting character exist, the traveler can start their adventure (UC-02).
4. Preparing the starting state again, or twice, never creates a second profile, a second character or a second solo group.

---

### Result:

The traveler is signed in and a traveler profile exists for this Google account. A returning traveler sees their group, campaign and today's quests. A new traveler sees the welcome screen while the starting state is being prepared; it will exist shortly.

---

### Business events made during the use case:

**TravelerRegistered**

- Created at 7a.1, as soon as the new traveler profile is saved (not at the end of the use case).
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that creates the solo group (7a.2). This is why preparing the starting state is async.
- Not created in the main flow or in 3a, 5a, 6a, 7b and 7c, because nothing was registered. In 9a it already exists.

**SoloGroupCreated**

- Created when the solo group is ready. The group has its own ID, which marks everything the traveler does in it.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that builds the starting character in this group (7a.2), and the app, which lets the traveler start once the character exists too (9a.3).

**CharacterCreated**

- Created when the starting character is ready.
- Reacts to it: the app, which lets the traveler start once the solo group exists too (9a.3).

Signing in itself creates no business event: other services react to `TravelerRegistered` and to completed quests, not to logins.

---

### Metrics produced in this use case:

- Sign-in success rate (successful vs failed sign-ins).
- Failed sign-ins by reason: cancelled or denied (5a), Google check failed (6a), profile lookup failed (7b), profile not saved (7c). 3a is invisible to the app: the traveler's browser never reaches us.
- Registration success rate (new travelers registered vs failed with 7c).
- Time from 7a.1 until both the solo group and the starting character are ready (how long travelers see "Your adventure is being prepared") - used to see if the speed of preparation and setup needs to be sped up.

"Sign-in failed" is a metric or log, not a business event: no other service reacts to it.
