# UC-02 Traveler starts a solo adventure

**Actor:** traveler who has no friends in the app yet (alone in their solo group) \
**Goal:** start a campaign with a chosen goal and get the first chapter \
**Preconditions:** Traveler is signed in (UC-01) **&** the solo group and the starting character exist **&** the group has no active campaign \
**Trigger:** Traveler sees "Start your adventure" after signing in, or after a previous campaign has ended.

---

### Main flow:

1. The app shows a short list of campaign goals, each with a theme for the story (for example the sea, the mountains, the forest).
2. Traveler chooses a goal.
3. The app confirms the start and tells the traveler that the Dungeon Master is preparing the first chapter.
4. The app saves the group's campaign with the chosen goal.
5. The Dungeon Master looks at the traveler's character (level, equipment, recent actions) and writes the opening chapter: the first obstacle on the way, for example the dark sea.
6. The app shows the opening chapter and today's quests: the default quests, and the traveler's own recurring activities (UC-05) that are due today, once the traveler has added some. The traveler can choose today's quest (UC-04).

The theme of the goal only shapes the story. It does not change which quests the traveler sees.

---

### Alternate flows:

**2a. Traveler leaves without choosing a goal** (at step 2)

1. No campaign is saved. The app offers the choice again the next time the traveler opens it. Use case ends.

**4a. Traveler confirms twice or retries** (at step 4)

1. The app never creates a second campaign for the same group. It continues with the campaign that already exists.

**4b. The app fails to save the campaign** (at step 4)

1. The app could not save the campaign (internal error).
2. The app shows a message that the adventure could not start. No campaign exists. The traveler can retry from step 2. Use case ends.

**5a. The Dungeon Master cannot be reached** (at step 5)

1. The outside service that writes the story does not answer in time, or returns an error.
2. The app uses a ready-made opening chapter for the chosen goal, so the traveler is not blocked. The chapter is marked as ready-made. The Dungeon Master takes over from the next chapter.
3. The use case continues at step 6.

**6a. Today's quests cannot be shown** (at step 6)

1. The part of the system that knows the quests does not answer.
2. The traveler sees the chapter with the message "Your quests are loading". The quests appear as soon as they are available.

---

### Result:

A campaign with the chosen goal exists for the traveler's solo group, the traveler has read the opening chapter, and can choose today's quest. The solo group has no side quests (UC-03), because the traveler is its only member. Friends can be invited later.

---

### Services involved:

- **story:** campaign, chapters, the Dungeon Master (calls the LLM, an external API).
- **character:** gives `story` the level, equipment and recent actions of the group's members.
- **quest:** shows today's quests (default quests and the traveler's own activities that are due).

---

### Business events made during the use case:

**CampaignStarted**

- Created at step 4, as soon as the campaign is saved.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that writes the opening chapter (step 5).
- Not created in 2a and 4b. In 4a it already exists.

**ChapterNarrated**

- Created at step 5, when the opening chapter is ready. Also created when the chapter is ready-made (5a).
- Reacts to it: the app, which shows the chapter (step 6).

"The Dungeon Master used a ready-made chapter" is a metric or log, not a business event: no other service reacts to it.

---

### Metrics produced in this use case:

- Campaigns started by goal.
- Time from step 4 until the opening chapter is shown.
- Share of opening chapters that were ready-made (5a): shows how often the outside story service fails.
- Duration and errors of calls to the outside story service (with the state of the circuit breaker).
- How often today's quests cannot be shown at step 6 (6a).
