# UC-03 Traveler adds a side quest for friends

**Actor:** traveler who adds the side quest (creator), and the other members of their group (friends) \
**Goal:** invite friends to join an activity within 24 hours, for extra XP (the creator gets no extra XP for it) \
**Preconditions:** Creator is signed in (UC-01) **&** the group has at least one other member **&** the creator has not reached the daily limit of side quests (3 per day, configurable) \
**Trigger:** Creator wants friends to join in, either in today's daily quest (for example "go for a run") or in something else (for example "come for a drink this evening").

---

### Main flow:

1. Creator starts a side quest in one of two ways: "Call other travelers" on today's chosen daily quest, or "Add side quest" for anything else.
2. With "Call other travelers", the activity is taken from the daily quest, and the XP of the side quest is a quarter of the daily quest's XP (rounded down, at least 1). With "Add side quest", the creator writes what the activity is and sets the XP of the side quest with a slider from 2 to 10. The range is small on purpose, so the XP cannot be inflated.
3. Creator confirms.
4. The app saves the side quest with its XP for the group. It is valid for 24 hours. The creator cannot complete it and gets no extra XP for it. If it comes from the creator's daily quest, the creator keeps the XP of that daily quest as usual (UC-04).
5. The other members of the group get a notification about the new side quest.
6. A friend opens the side quest and joins in real life.
7. Within 24 hours, the friend marks the side quest as done.
8. The app records the completion for this friend. The friend gets the XP of the side quest as extra XP. It comes on top of the friend's own daily quest, which stays as it is (UC-04), and it does not depend on it. The creator gets a notification that a friend joined.

Other friends can complete the same side quest until the 24 hours pass. Each friend can complete it once.

---

### Alternate flows:

**1a. Creator is alone in the group** (at step 1)

1. The app does not offer side quests to a traveler who is the only member of their group. Use case ends.

**1b. Creator has not chosen a daily quest today** (at step 1)

1. The app does not offer "Call other travelers". The creator can still use "Add side quest".

**3a. The daily limit is reached** (at step 3)

1. The creator already added the maximum number of side quests today.
2. The app tells the creator that the daily limit is reached. No side quest is saved. Use case ends.

**3b. Creator confirms twice or retries** (at step 3)

1. The app never saves a second side quest for the same request. Calling other travelers twice on the same daily quest creates only one side quest.

**3c. The XP is outside the allowed range** (at step 3, only for "Add side quest")

1. The XP is less than 2 or more than 10. The slider does not allow it, but the app checks it again when saving.
2. The app refuses and tells the creator the allowed range. Nothing is saved. The use case continues at step 2.

**4a. The app fails to save the side quest** (at step 4)

1. The app could not save the side quest (internal error).
2. The app shows a message that the side quest was not added. Nothing is saved and no friend is notified. The creator can retry from step 1. Use case ends.

**7a. The creator tries to complete their own side quest** (at step 7)

1. The app refuses and tells the creator that it is their own side quest. No XP is awarded.

**7b. The friend already completed the side quest** (at step 7)

1. The app does not record a second completion. No second XP is awarded.

**7c. The 24 hours have passed** (at step 7)

1. The side quest is expired.
2. The app tells the friend that the side quest has expired. No XP is awarded and nobody is penalised. Use case ends.

**8a. The XP cannot be awarded right away** (at step 8)

1. The completion is recorded, but the part of the system that awards XP is slow or not reachable.
2. The friend sees that the side quest is done and the XP is on its way. The XP arrives later and is never awarded twice.

**E1. Nobody completes the side quest** (24 hours after step 4)

1. The side quest expires on its own. The app marks it as expired and no longer lets anyone complete it.
2. No XP is awarded and nobody is penalised.

---

### Result:

The side quest exists for the group for 24 hours. Every friend who joined in time got the XP of the side quest once, the creator got a notification for each completion, and the creator got no extra XP. After 24 hours the side quest is expired, whether anybody completed it or not.

---

### Services involved:

- **quest:** daily quest, side quests and their XP, daily limit, completions, expiry after 24 hours.
- **character:** awards the XP to the friend (see UC-04).
- **notification:** tells the friends about the new side quest and the creator about completions.
- **group:** says who the other members of the group are.

---

### Business events made during the use case:

**SideQuestCreated**

- Created at step 4, as soon as the side quest is saved.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that notifies the group (step 5).
- Not created in 1a, 1b (only the first way is closed), 3a, 3b (already exists) and 4a.

**SideQuestCompleted**

- Created at step 8, once for every friend who completes it.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that awards XP to the friend, and the part that notifies the creator.
- Not created in 7a, 7b and 7c. In 8a it already exists.

**SideQuestExpired**

- Created when the 24 hours pass (E1, or 7c for the first time it is noticed). Created even when nobody completed the side quest.
- Reacts to it: the app, which stops offering the side quest.

A refused completion (7a, 7b, 7c) or a reached limit (3a) is a metric or log, not a business event: no other service reacts to it.

---

### Metrics produced in this use case:

- Side quests created per day, and how often the daily limit is reached (3a).
- Share of side quests that start from a daily quest compared with free side quests.
- Share of side quests completed by at least one friend within 24 hours.
- Time from creation until the first completion.
- Share of side quests that expired without any completion.
- Refused completions by reason: own side quest (7a), already completed (7b), expired (7c).
- Spread of the XP creators set on free side quests (the slider from 2 to 10).
