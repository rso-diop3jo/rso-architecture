# UC-04 Traveler completes a daily quest

**Actor:** traveler \
**Goal:** choose today's daily quest, do it and get XP, level, streak and trophies for it \
**Preconditions:** Traveler is signed in (UC-01) **&** the group has an active campaign (UC-02) **&** the traveler has not completed a daily quest today **&** the traveler is not resting \
**Trigger:** Traveler wants to do something for today's quest.

This is the complex use case: five of the six services take part.

---

### Main flow:

1. Traveler opens today's quests. The app shows the quest menu: the default quests, which are the same for everybody (for example 10,000 steps, take out the trash, call a family member), and the traveler's own recurring activities (UC-05) that are due today. An activity is due while it was done fewer times in the current day, week or month than its repeat says (for example vacuuming once a week stays on the menu until it is done once this week).
2. Traveler chooses one as today's daily quest. The group can see what the traveler chose.
3. Traveler does the activity in real life.
4. Traveler marks the daily quest as done.
5. The app saves the completion and tells the traveler right away that the quest is done and the reward is on its way. The traveler does not wait for the rest of the processing.
6. The traveler's character gets the XP of the quest (fixed for a default quest, rated when the activity was added for an own activity, UC-05), and the streak is updated.
7. If the XP crosses the limit for the next level, the character levels up. If a trophy rule is met (for example a 7-day streak), the character unlocks the trophy.
8. The group updates its leaderboard with the traveler's new XP.
9. The campaign adds the traveler's XP to the group's progress towards the goal.
10. The traveler gets a live message if there was a level up or a new trophy.
11. The app shows the updated character: XP, level, streak and trophies.

---

### Alternate flows:

**1a. The quests cannot be shown** (at step 1)

1. The part of the system that knows the quests does not answer.
2. The app shows a message that the quests are not available right now. The traveler can retry from step 1. Use case ends.

**2a. Traveler already chose today's quest** (at step 1 or 2)

1. The app shows today's quest instead of the menu and does not allow a second choice.
2. The use case continues at step 3.

**4a. The quest is already completed** (at step 4)

1. The app does not record a second completion. No second reward is given. The app shows the quest as done.

**4b. The quest is no longer available** (at step 4)

1. The day ended before the traveler marked the quest as done.
2. The app tells the traveler that this quest is no longer available. No reward is given and nobody is penalised. Use case ends.

**5a. The app fails to save the completion** (at step 5)

1. The app could not save the completion (internal error).
2. The app shows a message that the quest was not completed. Nothing is saved and no reward is given. The traveler can retry from step 4. Use case ends.

**6a. The character is slow or not reachable** (at step 6)

1. The completion is saved, but the part of the system that rewards the traveler does not react yet.
2. The traveler still sees "Quest done, reward is on its way" and is not blocked. The reward is applied as soon as the character is back, and it is never applied twice.

**6b. The same completion arrives twice** (at step 6)

1. The character recognises the completion it has already processed and skips it. XP, streak, level and trophies change only once.

**8a. The group, the campaign or the notifications are not reachable** (at step 8, 9 or 10)

1. The traveler's reward (steps 6 and 7) is not affected.
2. The leaderboard, the campaign progress or the message catch up as soon as the part is back.

---

### Result:

Today's quest is chosen and done once. The traveler's XP, level, streak and trophies are updated, the group's leaderboard and the campaign progress include the quest, and the traveler knows about a level up or a new trophy. If a part of the system is down for a moment, the completion is not lost and its effects arrive later.

---

### Services involved (5 of 6):

- **quest:** the quest menu, the choice and the completion (steps 1 to 5).
- **character:** XP, streak, level and trophies (steps 6 and 7). Its state is kept as a sequence of events (ADR-010).
- **group:** shows what each traveler chose today (step 2) and keeps the leaderboard (step 8).
- **story:** campaign progress towards the goal (step 9).
- **notification:** live message (step 10).

Only the sign-in before the use case (UC-01) involves the sixth service, `identity`.

---

### Business events made during the use case:

**DailyQuestChosen**

- Created at step 2, as soon as the choice is saved. Once per traveler per day.
- Reacts to it: the group, which shows what each traveler chose today.
- Not created in 1a. In 2a it already exists.

**QuestCompleted**

- Created at step 5, as soon as the completion is saved. Once per quest per traveler.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that rewards the traveler (steps 6 and 7).
- Not created in 4a, 4b and 5a. In 6a it already exists.

**XpAwarded**

- Created at step 6, when the character got the XP.
- Reacts to it: the leaderboard of the group (step 8) and the campaign progress (step 9).
- Created later than `QuestCompleted` in 6a. Not created twice in 6b.

**LevelUp**

- Created at step 7, only when the XP crosses the limit for the next level.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that sends the live message (step 10).

**TrophyUnlocked**

- Created at step 7, only when a trophy rule is met.
- Reacts to it: the part of the system that sends the live message (step 10).

The events the character keeps for itself (ADR-010) are not these. These are the events other services see.

---

### Metrics produced in this use case:

- Share of travelers who chose a quest today, and share of chosen quests that are completed.
- Chosen quests from the traveler's own list compared with default quests.
- Time from `QuestCompleted` until `XpAwarded` (how long the traveler waits for the reward; grows when the character is slow, 6a).
- Level ups and trophies per day.
- Duplicate completions skipped (6b).
- Refused or failed completions by reason: already completed (4a), no longer available (4b), not saved (5a).
- Time until the leaderboard and the campaign progress include the quest (8a).
